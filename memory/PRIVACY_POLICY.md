# Privacy Policy

**Toolbox Vault**

**Effective Date:** May 16, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 16, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Toolbox Vault mobile application
("Toolbox Vault", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your
information when you use our mobile application (the "App") on iOS and Android
devices.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of
information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Email address and password (stored encrypted) when
  you create an account.
- **Tool & Inventory Data:** Names, photos, model numbers, purchase dates,
  prices, locations, dealers, warranty info, receipts, and any other content
  you choose to enter about your personal tools and assets.
- **Dealer & Contact Information:** Dealer business names, agent names,
  phone numbers, and email addresses you choose to add.
- **Borrower / Contact Information:** Names and phone numbers of people you
  check tools out to, either typed manually or imported from your device's
  Contacts app with your explicit permission.
- **Photos & Receipts:** Images of your tools and purchase receipts that you
  upload through the camera or photo library.
- **Subscription Information:** Subscription tier, purchase date, and renewal
  status (processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store — we never
  see your payment card details).
- **Feedback & Support Messages:** Any feedback or bug reports you submit
  through the in-app form.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Crash & Performance Data:** Anonymous diagnostic logs that help us
  identify and fix bugs. No personal content is included.
- **App Usage Events:** Aggregate counts of which features are used most.
  Not tied to your identity.

### 2.3 Information We Do **Not** Collect

- **Location data** of any kind.
- **Microphone audio.**
- **Health, fitness, or medical data.**
- **Browsing history** outside the App.
- **Financial information** (handled solely by Apple/Google).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core functionality
- Sync your inventory data securely across your devices
- Process and verify your subscription
- Respond to support requests and feedback
- Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.
We do **not** use your information for targeted advertising.

## 4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We share information **only** in these limited cases:

- **Apple / Google In-App Purchase:** Subscription purchases are processed by
  Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive only a receipt confirmation —
  not your payment card details.
- **RevenueCat:** Our subscription management provider. They receive an
  anonymous user identifier and your subscription status. See
  https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/
- **Hosting Provider:** Your encrypted data is stored on servers operated by
  our cloud hosting provider. They have no access to readable content.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by a valid court order or law
  enforcement request.

## 5. Data Storage & Security

- All data in transit is encrypted with industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.
- Passwords are stored using one-way hashing (bcrypt). We can never see your
  password in readable form.
- Photos and receipts are stored on secure servers with restricted access.
- We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete
  your account at any time from **More → Account → Delete Account**, which
  immediately and permanently removes all your data from our servers.

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access** the information we have about you (visible in-app at any time)
- **Correct** or update any information directly in the App
- **Delete** your entire account and all associated data via
  **More → Account → Delete Account**
- **Export** your data via **More → Import / Export → Export CSV**
- **Revoke** Contacts permission at any time in your device's Settings — this
  has no effect on data already saved in your borrowers list (you can
  delete those manually in-app)
- **Cancel** your subscription at any time through Apple App Store or Google
  Play Store

If you live in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have
additional rights including the right to data portability and the right to
object to processing. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ but is **not** intended for use by children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If
you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us
immediately and we will delete it.

## 8. Permissions Requested

The App will ask for the following permissions:

| Permission | Used For | Optional? |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | Taking photos of tools and receipts | Yes |
| **Photo Library** | Selecting existing photos from your gallery | Yes |
| **Contacts** | Importing borrower names and phone numbers when checking out a tool | Yes |
| **Face ID / Touch ID** | Optional secure sign-in (you choose to enable) | Yes |
| **Notifications** | Optional reminders for warranty expirations and maintenance due dates | Yes |

You may decline any of these and continue using the App. None are required
for core functionality.

## 9. Subscription Auto-Renewal

If you purchase a subscription:

- Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at purchase
  confirmation
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before
  the end of the current period
- Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end
  of the current period at the price you originally agreed to
- You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID or Google Play
  account settings after purchase

will always be available within the App and at this URL. Material changes
will be notified via an in-app banner at next launch.
