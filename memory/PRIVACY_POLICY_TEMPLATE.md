## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** email address and password (the password is stored
  only as a one-way bcrypt hash — we cannot read or recover it)
- **Inventory data you create:** tool details, photos, receipts, dealer info,
  borrower contact details, maintenance records, warranty claims, notes
- **Promo codes** you redeem
- **Feedback / support messages** you send us

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device & app data:** app version, operating-system version, language,
  approximate region (from your device, not GPS)
- **Crash and performance logs** (no personally-identifying information)
- **Subscription status** received from Apple, Google, and our payment
  processor (see §4)

### 2.3 Information we do NOT collect
- We do **not** collect your precise location (GPS).
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
- We do **not** show third-party advertising in the App.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:
1. Operate, maintain, and improve the App.
2. Authenticate you and protect your account.
3. Sync your inventory data across your devices.
4. Process subscription purchases, renewals, refunds, and cancellations.
5. Send you transactional emails (e.g., password reset codes).
6. Diagnose technical issues and prevent abuse.
7. Comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** use your inventory data, photos, or receipts to train AI models
or for any purpose other than providing the App's features to you.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We share the minimum necessary information with the following service
providers, each under contracts that require them to protect your data.

| Provider | Why we share with them | What is shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | To process subscription purchases | Anonymous purchase token + your subscription status |
| **RevenueCat** | To verify your subscription entitlement and keep status in sync across devices | Anonymous user ID, purchase receipts, entitlement status |
| **OpenAI** (GPT-4o Vision) | Only when you choose to scan a receipt — extracts text from the image you submit | The single receipt image you submit; **OpenAI does not retain it for training** under our API terms |
| **MongoDB Atlas** (database hosting) | To store your inventory data | All data you create in the App, encrypted at rest |
| **[Email provider — e.g., SendGrid]** | To deliver password-reset and feedback emails | Your email address and the email body |

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored on encrypted servers operated by our infrastructure provider.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; we never store or transmit them in plain
  text.
- All API traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS.
- Photos and receipts you upload are stored encrypted at rest.

No system is 100% secure. We follow industry best practices but cannot
guarantee absolute security.

your personal information.
