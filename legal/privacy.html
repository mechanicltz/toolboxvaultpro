Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 8, 2025
Last updated: December 8, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Toolbox Vault ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Who We Are

Toolbox Vault is operated by Ryan Overby ("Company"). For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at MechanicVault@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and password (the password is stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash — we cannot read or recover it)
  • Inventory data you create: tool details, photos, receipts, dealer info, borrower contact details, maintenance records, warranty claims, notes
  • Promo codes you redeem
  • Feedback / support messages you send us

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & app data: app version, operating-system version, language, approximate region (from your device, not GPS)
  • Crash and performance logs (no personally-identifying information)
  • Subscription status received from Apple, Google, and our payment processor (see §4)

2.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your precise location (GPS).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not show third-party advertising in the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  1. Operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  2. Authenticate you and protect your account.
  3. Sync your inventory data across your devices.
  4. Process subscription purchases, renewals, refunds, and cancellations.
  5. Send you transactional emails (e.g., password reset codes).
  6. Diagnose technical issues and prevent abuse.
  7. Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your inventory data, photos, or receipts to train AI models or for any purpose other than providing the App's features to you.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on encrypted servers operated by our infrastructure provider.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; we never store or transmit them in plain text.
  • All API traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Photos and receipts you upload are stored encrypted at rest.

No system is 100% secure. We follow industry best practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we have about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format (JSON).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

You can exercise the delete and export rights directly inside the App: More → ACCOUNT → Delete Account or More → IMPORT/EXPORT → Export. For any other request, email MechanicVault@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

CCPA (California residents)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

GDPR (EU/UK residents)

The legal basis for our processing is contractual necessity (to deliver the Service you signed up for) and your consent. You may file a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have data-protection laws different from your home country. We use standard contractual clauses where applicable.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account and inventory data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days, except for limited records we are legally required to keep (e.g., billing records).

10. Subscription Information

The App offers paid subscriptions. Subscription pricing, billing, and auto-renewal are governed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play, depending on the device. See our Terms of Service for full details. To cancel:

  • iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Toolbox Vault → Cancel
  • Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App and/or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document tells you when the policy was last changed.
